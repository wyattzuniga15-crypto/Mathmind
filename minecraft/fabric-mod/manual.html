Everything in the mod, and how to use it

One hundred and sixty-five items and eight creatures. Most of them break the world; a fair number put it back. Every recipe below is the one the mod actually ships — this page is generated from the mod's own files, so it cannot drift from what is in the jar.

165Items
8Creatures
29Sculptures

Finding them

They are all in one creative tab called Orbital Arsenal. In survival, craft them — every recipe is below.

Using them

Hold the item and right-click. Most aim where you are looking, up to about a hundred blocks. Nearly all have a cooldown, so nothing can be spammed.

Undoing them

Every block this mod changes is recorded. A Rewind Clock puts the last thirty seconds back; the longer clocks go further, and the Genesis Clock goes back to the start.

Building by command

/build lists what it can raise — house, tower, castle, bridge, dome, pyramid, wall. It goes up where you stand, and the clocks undo it.

The companion

/ai spawn calls it, /ai <message> talks to it. /ai provider ollama points it at a model running on your own machine, so no key is needed.

A warning

Several of these permanently reshape hundreds of blocks. Try them somewhere you do not mind losing, or keep a clock in your other hand.
